# MPI-1039 - Multi-angle Flow

One picture becomes several shots of the same subject from other camera angles, via an image edit model.
Scope widened 2026-10-22 (Fabio): characters as well as places.

Four cases:
1. a character
2. new angles of that same character - orbit the camera round it, camera up / down - face and body angles
3. a room or scenery
4. new angles of that same room - camera left, right, up, down

The work is finding the model and the prompt. Exact camera placement from real geometry is MPI-623 (3D Scene, done).
