# LTX-2.5 Video — what ships, why, and what it cannot do yet

> The `ltx-25` card (MPI-1095). Dev-gated (`devOnly: true`) until stage 1 releases; it sits
> BESIDE LTX 2.3 Balanced, not in place of it. Bench evidence and raw sweeps live in the card's
> `research/findings.md` and `validation.md`; this page keeps only the durable facts.

## Why 2.5 at all

Fabio's verdict, 2026-10-22, on an 8-case side-by-side bench (same prompts and seeds as 2.3):
**acting is far better** — actors' motion and expressions are far more natural. Picture quality
is about the same. 2.5 is also faster on a 4060 Ti 16 GB, model warm,
960x512 5 s: **87-110 s vs 146-166 s** for 2.3 (stage 1 ~2x faster per step, stage 2 ~25%).
Part of that gap is 2.3's three baked LoRAs, which 2.5 does not carry.

The same tier bench retired **2.3 High** (bf16): it lost to both Balanced and 2.5 on time and
looked no better. Its weight stays in `modelDeps.js` marked DEPRECATED so the orphan sweep can
reclaim it from users' disks (never delete a dep entry — `docs/playbooks/add-model/README.md`).

## The graph

`comfy_workflows/ltx25_i2v_t2v.json`, generated by `generate_ltx25.py` from
`raw/ltx25_i2v_t2v_template.json`. It is the 2.3 Balanced (int8) graph with:

- six loaders re-pointed to the 2.5 files below;
- `DualCLIPLoader` → plain `CLIPLoader`: Gemma 4 carries its own text projection;
- the three baked 2.3 LoRAs (Merged, Transition, talk ID) **removed**. Fabio, 2026-10-22: no
  2.3 LoRAs on 2.5. No 2.5 builds of Transition or a talking-head ID LoRA exist (checked HF);
- the video VAE behind a Turbo switch (§ Turbo below).

One tier (int8), so nothing is stamped. Same ops as 2.3: `t2v_ms`, `i2v_ms` (start, end, both),
`ref2v_ms` (Ingredients), both audio modes, preview → Finish. Same progress bars as 2.3
(`progressStages.js`). Same resolution ladder (`tiers.md`, one `LTX_RATIOS` table for both cards).

Runs on the SHIPPED engine: ComfyUI v0.42.1 already has 2.5 support (core `57ce8e1a`), and the
ComfyUI-LTXVideo pin `3b9c5cde` runs the graph. No engine or node bump.

## Turbo = which video decoder (2026-10-23)

`Input_is_Turbo` → `MpiIfElse` → two `VAELoader`s. The If/Else is lazy, so a run loads ONE VAE
(checked in the app: Turbo on loaded only the conv file, Turbo off only `CausalDiffusionVAE`).
The card arms the shared `h3Turbo` button with `capabilities.h3TurboToggle: true`; default OFF.

- **OFF (default) — diffusion VAE ("DiffVAE").** Steadier fine texture: on Fabio's 2K dinosaur
  (2560x1472, 96 frames) the conv decoder's scales go blobby for a stretch of frames and recover;
  the DiffVAE keeps the scale pattern. Decode 164-173 s at that size, ~+60-70 s over conv
  (estimated, conv not timed in isolation). Peak VRAM ~15.9 GB of 16 at 2K.
- **ON — conv VAE.** The faster decode; the 2.5 original.

The DiffVAE also ENCODES the start frame (the guide nodes share the switched VAE), so the same
seed gives slightly different motion between the two settings. That is expected, not a bug.

Same steps either way: unlike H3's Turbo, nothing about sampling changes. The button's tooltip
still reads H3's "6 steps instead of 25" — owed a per-model line in `PromptBoxControls.js`.

## Audio

Both audio modes ship on 2.5 with NO LoRA:
- **Original** (attached audio drives the clip): lip-syncs on i2v with no Transition LoRA.
  2.3 needed Transition forced on for this (`audioMode` in `PromptBoxControls.js`).
- **Reference** (voice clone): Fabio judged the clone close enough with no ID-LoRA, though core
  `LTXVReferenceAudio` describes itself as an ID-LoRA node. On one seed 2.5 also cut to a new
  angle mid-clip (native multi-shot) — watch for it.

## Known limits

- **Fast entry morphs texture.** A subject that rushes in during the first second (the dinosaur)
  breaks up its fine texture and then settles. The VAE does not fix it: it is motion inside LTX's
  8-frame temporal groups being averaged. The candidate fix is De-RoPE (MPI-707, slow-motion the hot
  span, re-diffuse, drop the frames). Cost scales faster than the frames: on H3 2.2x frames cost
  3.8x per step, so aim it at the span and the subject with a mask — never the whole clip.
- **Foley stays on 2.3.** Lightricks has not shipped a 2.5 Foley LoRA; the Foley Flow requires
  `ltx-23-balanced`. All LTX Flows still require 2.3 Balanced (stage 2 moves them).
- **t2v framing drift:** one bench t2v ignored "medium shot" and went wide. One seed, not a rule.

## Benching a captured graph (trap)

A graph captured from engine history for an A/B names its start picture as
`input/mpi_staged/<hash>.png`. The app DELETES that file after the run, and `MpiLoadImage` with
`block_if_empty: false` then feeds a blank 1x1 and the run silently becomes t2v (a different scene,
exit 0). A path outside ComfyUI's input/output/temp also loads as missing. Copy the picture into
`input/<subfolder>/` and point the loader there; check the loader's `executed` event carries a
preview image before trusting the run. Cost two 9-minute runs, 2026-10-23.
